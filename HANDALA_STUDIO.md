## Handala Studio — timeline editor

Open the first experimental multi-track editor at **/studio.html** after starting the app. It includes local media import, video/image/audio lanes, clip selection, trim in/out, clip positioning, split-at-playhead, text overlay, aspect-ratio presets, audio volume, and browser-side WebM export. Media files remain local to the browser; only editor preferences are saved in local storage. This is an early-access editor: it is not yet feature-equivalent to CapCut, and advanced transitions, keyframes, captions, proxy media, and robust multi-clip audio/video compositing are future work.

The existing Reel Engine AI workflow remains available at the root path.
